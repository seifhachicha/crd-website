The company

Built by a specialist, not a generalist

CRD's story begins in 2004, carried by Mohamed Hachicha and his passion for the world of toys. After a Master's in Game and Toy Sciences at Paris 11 University, he built his expertise at the heart of this industry.

With more than 35 years of experience, Mohamed Hachicha is today an expert and unifying figure in the sector, forging lasting ties between brands, distributors and partners, and helping the toy ecosystem flourish.

Our mission is to democratise high-quality, educational games and toys for Tunisian youth, empowering parents to support their children's well-being and development.

We deliberately weigh the social, economic and environmental impact of what we distribute, and favour products that promote educational learning.
